# Why chains — from the measured difference to what you train

*Draft 2026-10-21 for Peter to verify. Method (Peter): "How do the best players have an RL 15% more
often? Because they die less. Why do they die less? Better 1v1 fighting, better decisions on how to get a
stack and where, and leaving a fight before they are at risk. How do they know that? Because they do the
following, and they train the following." Each chain below starts at a measured difference from
`research_top_na_fours_2026-10.md` and goes down until it reaches something a player can practise. Coaching
text speaks only from the bottom rungs. Status per chain: **to verify** until Peter says it holds.*

## The basics at the bottom of every chain
These are the only things a player can actually train. Every chain ends in one or more of them.

1. **Aim and movement** — dmm4 duels, POV review of your own demos, the strafe trainer (Denver :29001).
2. **Sound recognition** — the pickup sound of each item (red, yellow, green, mega, RL, LG, quad, pent),
   weapon fire by type and direction, jumps, teleporter, doors and lifts, water. No footsteps in QW.
3. **Item timing** — armors 20 s, weapons 30 s, mega 20 s after the holder is back at 100, quad 60 s,
   pent 5 min; count from the sound; arrive 2 s early, not 2 s late.
4. **Spawn routes** — from each spawn point (the 33 named ones), the gun first, then the stack, in order,
   and which rooms off that spawn are traps.
5. **Reading** — MM2 lines, the kill feed (a teammate's death names a lost room), the scoreboard when it
   shows stacks.
6. **Fight discipline** — hit first; when hit, answer or leave, never trade naked; the three leave triggers
   (his gun and stack beat yours, a second enemy's sound or call, the item you came for is gone); never
   walk a shotgun into a room your team just called lost.
7. **Communication** — the KTX MM2 macros bound; the spawn call (`need armor weapon @ loc`), the loss call
   (`lost loc 2>`), the item call (`took ra` / `enemy took ra`).

## Chain 1 — the rocket launcher
- **Difference:** the top hold an RL or LG 48% of their alive time, the field 32%.
- **Why?** Two things: they die less, so an RL kept is a life that lasts; and they reach a gun sooner after
  spawning.
- **Why do they die less?** (a) they win more of the 1v1s they are in — they hit first and, when hit, they
  answer or leave instead of trading shots with a shotgun; (b) they make better stack decisions — what is
  up at this second, the route to it, gun before armor; (c) they leave fights before they are at risk — the
  room whose RL is 20 s out, the moment a second enemy shows.
- **How do they know?** They hear the pickup and the fire and place the enemy; they count the timers and
  arrive early; they know from every spawn the order gun → stack; they read the MM2 and the kill feed.
- **Train:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 6.

## Chain 2 — empty lives (lives with no damage, frag or item; 7 more per game in the field)
- **Why?** Off the spawn the field runs at the nearest fight with the shotgun instead of taking something.
- **Why?** At that second they do not know what is up, and nobody told them, so the fight is the only thing
  they can see.
- **Why?** Timing is not trained to the second, and the spawn call is not a habit.
- **Train:** 3, 4, 7; the rule "take the item on the clock, then fight".

## Chain 3 — dying stacked without a gun (almost twice a game in the field, mostly dm2)
- **Why?** They pick up the armor that is on the way, then walk around with 150 and a shotgun, or stand
  waiting for the weapon to respawn.
- **Why?** Gun-then-stack is not automatic from that spawn, and the weapon's 30 s is not being counted.
- **Train:** 4 (gun first), 3 (count the weapon; if it is 20 s out, go to the other weapon or the LG,
  do not wait), 6.

## Chain 4 — landing the first hit (61% of fights vs 50%)
- **Why?** They see the enemy before he sees them.
- **Why?** They enter rooms by the angle that shows the doorway first, pre-aim the entrance, and already
  know which door he is coming through from the pickup or the fire they heard.
- **Why?** Map knowledge of the entries, sound, and aim good enough that the first shot lands.
- **Train:** 1, 2, the approach habit per room ("see the doorway first; fire from the entrance and back
  off, never walk onto him").

## Chain 5 — quad (the field walks out of the room in the last 10 s, 54% vs 43%)
- **Why?** They stop counting, get pulled into a fight, or will not sit naked in the room.
- **Why?** The 60 s is not counted to the second, nobody calls it, and they arrive without a gun.
- **Train:** 3 (count from the pickup sound), 7 ("quad in 10"), 4 (the gun before the room), one teammate
  assigned to it.

## Chain 6 — locked spawns (everyone dies the same; the top's spawns produce an item or damage)
- **Why?** Under a lock the top go for the item on the clock and put shotgun damage on whoever they pass;
  the field runs at the fight or hides.
- **Why?** They know what is up at that second and where it is from this spawn point; the field does not.
- **Train:** 3, 4, 6 (the trade rule: damage before you die), 7 (the loss call, so the next spawn knows).

## Chain 7 — alone into two (the death that defines omicron's night)
- **Why?** They did not know the second enemy was there, or knew and committed anyway.
- **Why?** They missed the kill feed (a teammate just died in that room), missed the `lost x 2>` call, or
  did not separate the second set of sounds.
- **Train:** 5, 2, 6 ("one shot from cover, then leave").

## Chain 8 — the MM2 line nobody sends
- **Why?** NA plays on voice; the macros are not bound; the habit was never built.
- **Train:** 7 — bind the macros, make the spawn call and the loss call automatic.

## How the coaching is built from this
- A Game Report moment names the rung that failed (for example "you walked a shotgun into a room your
  team had just called lost: fight discipline, trigger two") and the drill for it.
- The coach's read of a typed answer grades the read against the chain: did the player see the clock,
  the MM2, the route, the leave trigger?
- The coach Overview levers become chains, not numbers: "you are dying stacked without a gun — chain 3 —
  this week: gun first off every dm2 spawn, count the RL".
